## App Reviewメモ（英語）

No login is required.

Review steps:
1. Launch the app. The title screen closes automatically after about two seconds.
2. Choose Fishing, Outing, or Work.
3. Tap checklist items and confirm that the progress bar updates.
4. Use Temporary Save, Clear, and Restore to confirm local state handling.
5. Open the Templates tab to create or rename a template, select one of 12 icons, reorder items, and add or remove items.
6. On the list-selection or template-editing screen, use the optional Remove Ads purchase or Restore Purchase control. Product ID: `jp.khunryo.forgetcheck.removeads`.

The checklist screen, progress screen, temporary save/restore flow, and launch screen do not display ads. A small Google AdMob adaptive banner is requested only on the checklist-selection and template-editing screens. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized (`npa: true`) and UMP consent handling runs before an ad request. If an ad is unavailable, all checklist features continue to work.

Checklist items, template names, completion states, and temporary snapshots remain in on-device storage. They are not sent to the developer or advertising provider. The app has no account, cloud sync, external analytics SDK, subscription, or external AI service. The only in-app purchase is the optional non-consumable ad-removal product. StoreKit verifies its entitlement without sending purchase history to a developer server.
